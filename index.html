Mahanaim Academy · Camp OG God

School of Excellence.

A Christian mission-oriented school in Sagnerigu Kukuo, Tamale, committed to accessible, quality education for every child of school-going age in Ghana.

Our vision

The Mahanaim Academy School is a Christian mission-oriented school that strives for excellence in the provision of accessible and quality education to all children of school-going-age in Ghana.

Our mission

To provide access and quality education without discrimination, while developing each child's intellectual, moral, social, religious and cultural capabilities.

Our promise

To help harness Ghana's future human resource base so that every product of the school contributes positively to national development.
